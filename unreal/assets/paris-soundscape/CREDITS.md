Source URLs, original download SHA-256 values and recording notes are pinned in `sources.json`.
The generated files are converted to mono 48 kHz PCM16, trimmed, gain adjusted and faded or crossfaded. They are not unmodified masters.

The Freesound inputs use the publicly provided HQ preview files; these are compressed previews, not the original lossless masters. Recording and placement provenance remain distinct: the market recording is from Bastille and is reused as an appearance proxy near this district's mapped shops. Pigeon locations and all event timing are inferred. No actual emergency, business opening status or Notre-Dame service schedule is represented.

The bell is a historic recording before the 2019 fire. It is an excerpt of the Emmanuel bourdon, not a synthetic imitation or a live feed.
The French siren source is an authored reproduction of a two-tone siren, not a field recording of an emergency vehicle in Paris.
