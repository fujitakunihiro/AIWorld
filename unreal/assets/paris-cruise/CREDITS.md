# Cruise vessel source

**Small passenger ferry, 25 m** by Innerscene, published as
[CC0 / public domain](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/small-passenger-ferry-25-m-bb6ba356).
The download URL and SHA-256 of the reviewed GLB are recorded in `manifest.json`.

The source is an unbranded passenger ferry, used here as a sightseeing vessel;
it is not a scan of a particular Paris boat. We convert its coordinates and
materials for UE, estimate its waterline, add an original wake and animate it
along an inferred safe path within the existing OSM river polygon. The source
model's overall dimensions and authored 2K colour maps are retained.
