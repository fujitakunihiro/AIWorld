# Sedan appearance source

BMW model by **Mike Pan**, corrected and exported by **Morgan McGuire**.
Downloaded from [Morgan McGuire's Computer Graphics Archive](https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/index.html).
The pinned [asset metadata](https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/research/model/bmw/info.js) identifies the 2019-04-17 revision as **CC0/Public Domain**.
License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The source and exact checksums are recorded in `manifest.json`. Source archives
are cached locally in `Raw/`; a verified cache permits an offline rebuild.

Modifications in AI WORLD: uniform 4.2m length to fit the existing simulated
traffic safety envelope; UE axis conversion; split rotating wheels; zero-area
face removal; authored automotive PBR materials, colour variants, fictional
French-style number plates and brake-lamp control. These are visual additions
to inferred traffic. The model does not identify a real vehicle observed in Paris.

No endorsement by the vehicle manufacturer is implied.
